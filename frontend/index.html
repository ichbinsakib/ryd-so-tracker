    What happens next

    1. It opens as a part check (status 1. ANALYSIS).
    2. Steel type, bars, feet, pounds and stock on hand fill in from STEEL USAGE and the STOCK list.
    3. Request the material quote, record it when it arrives, and compare its price.
    4. Then use Request SO on the page to enter the SO#, due date and type. It becomes 3.OPEN.
